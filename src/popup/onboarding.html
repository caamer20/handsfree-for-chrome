Your browser. At your word.

Try it, one step at a time.

1. Check your microphone

After enabling it above, check that your chosen microphone picks up sound.

2. Try your first browser command

Open a blank practice tab in the background. This checks browser control without requiring a microphone or AI key.

3. Try it by voice

Open a regular website, press your shortcut, and say “open a new tab”. Check the extension’s Control screen for the result. Press the shortcut again to stop listening.

For field editing or dictation, allow that website in Settings → Page controls. Typing a command in Control always remains available.

Something not working?

Check your shortcut, saved permissions, and engine state. This makes no AI request.

    You choose how to interpret commands.

    Built-in commands and the bundled AI run locally. You can add your own cloud AI provider and API key in Settings. Chrome’s speech service may send audio to Google while listening and need internet. HandsFree has no analytics. You can always type a command instead.
