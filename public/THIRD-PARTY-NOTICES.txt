The browser bundle excludes the Node.js-only sharp image-processing backend.
An override to sharp 0.35.4 addresses its transitive development/runtime audit findings.

The HandsFree icon was generated with an AI image-generation tool.
Its generation prompt is provided in the source repository's assets/ICON-PROMPT.md.
